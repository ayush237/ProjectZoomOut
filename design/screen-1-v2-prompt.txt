Revise the Track roadmap screen. The structure and the token usage are right - keep the meandering spine, the four node states, the header, and the Continue pill. Three corrections. FIRST, the path is too wide: narrow the meander so the spine stays within roughly the middle two thirds of the frame instead of swinging to both edges. SECOND, each node needs a small preview of its Leaf rather than a bare number - the next node keeps its full title beneath it, and completed and locked nodes carry a short truncated label near them, small and muted, so the reader can see the shape of the book without opening it; keep locked titles vague enough not to spoil, a few words at most. THIRD, and most important: the graph currently reads as a CONSTELLATION, not a neuron, because almost every connection is a straight line. Fix that everywhere. Every connection curves - no straight segments anywhere in the screen, including the faint background web; use quadratic or cubic bezier paths with varying curvature rather than line segments between points. Dendrites must branch RECURSIVELY and TAPER, three or four levels deep, each level thinner and shorter than the last, rather than radiating as straight spokes from a centre - that spoke pattern is the main thing making it look artificial. Add many fine terminal branches at the extremities; density out there is most of what reads as organic. Make the dendritic field ASYMMETRIC around each node, because radial symmetry reads as a snowflake. Give each node body a slightly irregular rounded shape rather than a perfect circle. And give each cell one process that is noticeably longer and thinner than the rest, like an axon, so the cell has a direction. The result should look like a stained neuron under a microscope, not a star chart. Keep every colour, font, radius and spacing value from the ZoomOut design system exactly as they are.
