## dinner_table_knowledge

The deep cut — the detail a reader repeats to someone else that evening. Surprising,
specific, and **from this book**.

**It cannot be emitted without a sourced claim on the `takeaway` slide.** Not as a matter of
form: this is the field most likely to be repeated aloud as fact about a named author, which
makes it the field where an invention does the most damage. If the passages do not carry
something genuinely surprising, **return nothing for this field.** That is a correct answer,
and a common one.

Do not reach for biography, publication history, or anything about the author's life — you
have the book's text and nothing else, so anything of that kind would be invented.

## apply_in_life

**One concrete thing the reader could do tomorrow.** A specific action in an ordinary day,
not an intention and not a mindset. "Notice when you…" is not an action. "Before your next
meeting, write down…" is.

It must follow from this Leaf's concept rather than being generic self-improvement advice.
If the concept does not translate into an action, return nothing.

## claims

Every factual assertion about what the book says needs an entry in **claims**, with a
`passage_ref` you were given, a `note`, and — only if you can copy it exactly — a `quote`.
The Dinner Table fact in particular must appear here with `slide_key` set to `takeaway`.
