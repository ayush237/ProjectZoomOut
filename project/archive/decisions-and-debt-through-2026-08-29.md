| 2026-08-29 | **Two retry layers multiply — when wrapping a client that already retries, one layer must be turned off** | WP20's throughput collapse, root-caused. The pipeline's own retry logic sat on top of the Vertex SDK's, so N SDK attempts inside M of ours is N×M calls and a wall clock that is the *product* of two backoff schedules, not the sum. **109 minutes of a two-hour run were spent inside calls that had not yet returned** — which is why nothing logged an error and why it read as model latency: a stalled call is not a failed call, and no layer was reporting anything. Fixed with `attempts=1` on the SDK. **Architect's quota-backoff hypothesis was wrong**; the pattern was exponential because backoff is exponential, not because a 429 was involved. **The rule is general and applies to every client this project wraps**: before adding retry logic, establish whether the layer beneath already has it, and disable one. The symptom of getting it wrong is silence and slowness, not errors — the hardest shape to diagnose | Keeping both layers with tuned caps (the product still grows faster than either factor); removing the pipeline's own retries instead (the SDK's is less controllable and does not know about the pipeline's idempotency) |
